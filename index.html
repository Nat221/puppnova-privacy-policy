Privacy Policy for PuppNova

Last updated: October 8, 2026

PuppNova ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PuppNova mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our policies regarding user data and privacy when using our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

No Personal Data Collected: PuppNova does not require user accounts, logins, or registration. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or payment details).

Local Device Storage: Any preferences saved inside the app (such as favoriting products) are stored strictly on your local device using local storage mechanisms. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded or transmitted to any external server maintained by us.

2. External Shopping & Affiliate Links

PuppNova lists dog products and supplies sourced from external online retailers (such as Amazon.com). When you tap on a product link inside PuppNova, you are redirected to the official external website of that retailer to view or complete your purchase.

Please note that PuppNova participates in affiliate marketing programs. Tapping an affiliate link may pass a referral tag to the retailer's site so we can earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. We do not process transactions or collect payment details inside PuppNova. Once redirected, your interactions are governed by the respective retailer's privacy policy.

3. Third-Party Services

PuppNova does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytics tracking tools, or user profiling frameworks.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for general audiences and adult pet owners (aged 18 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this web page with an updated revision date.
